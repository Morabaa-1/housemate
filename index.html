The problem

In many shared houses, one person pays the bills. Then they chase everyone on WhatsApp.

  • Nobody is sure what they owe. The numbers are lost in the chat.
  • One person does all the work. They do the maths and send the reminders.
  • Asking friends for money is awkward. Again and again.

How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Add a bill

Say what it is, how much, and who shares it.

2

It splits the bill

Everyone pays the same share. The total always adds up.

3

Pay and confirm

You tap "I've paid". The person who paid the bill taps "Confirm received".

Every payment has a clear status

  • Not paidNobody has paid yet.
  • Awaiting confirmationYou said you paid. Waiting for the other person to check.
  • PaidThe person who paid the bill got the money.

Every bill shows the sum, so you can see why you owe what you owe.

Good to know

  • This is a student demo. It does not move real money and does not connect to any bank.
  • Your changes stay in your own browser. They do not appear on your housemates' phones.
  • Each bill is tracked on its own. If you and a housemate owe each other, both amounts are shown.
  • You can tap "Reset demo" at any time to start again.
